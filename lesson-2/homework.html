3. Present Continuous

Complete the sentences. Use Present Continuous.

Раскрой скобки, поставив глаголы в Present Continuous. Не забудь выбрать am / is / are и добавить -ing к глаголу.

Remember!

I → amHe / She / It → isYou / We / They → are
Example:

Tom ___ (play) tennis now.

Tom is playing tennis now.
